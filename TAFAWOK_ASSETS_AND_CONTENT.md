## 3. Health, Safety & Environment (HSE) Policy

Approved and overseen by **TAFAWOK Executive Leadership**, TAFAWOK enforces strict HSE standards across all construction sites, facilities, and regional operations:

### Policy Statement:

> _"TAFAWOK aims to achieve the highest standards of Health, Safety and Environment by incorporating the principles of sustainable improvement throughout its business activities."_

### Key HSE Commitments & Principles:

- **Zero Accidents Target:** Strive to achieve an accident-free workplace by complying strictly with national and international health and safety Acts, Regulations, and OSHA guidelines.
- **Stakeholder Protection:** Protect employees, subcontractors, client representatives, and interested third parties from occupational injury and health hazards.
- **Risk Assessment & Mitigation:** Conduct continuous Occupational Health and Safety risk identification, opportunity assessments, and timely mitigations before high-risk execution.
- **Hazard Identification:** Systematically identify and control all potential hazards in TAFAWOK operations through risk analysis and job safety analysis (JSA).
- **Environmental Preservation:** Prevent environmental pollution by minimizing site waste, lowering carbon intensity, and protecting natural resources.
- **Emergency Preparedness:** Conduct mandatory Emergency Mock Drills to train site personnel to respond swiftly and safely to emergencies.
- **Continuous Competency Training:** Provide continuous HSE training programs to systematically raise the safety competency level of all site personnel.
- **Consultation & Safety Culture:** Foster an inclusive safety culture encouraging direct reporting of hazards and active consultation.
- **Incident Prevention:** Ensure all incidents and near-misses are thoroughly analyzed to implement root-cause prevention.
- **Universal Responsibility:** Instill that safety is an integral duty of everyday work: _"Every employee shares in the commitment to eliminate unsafe acts and conditions by thinking safely and acting safely at all times."_

## 10. Critical Content Audit & Website Modernization Plan

### 10.1 Key Weaknesses in Legacy Website

1. **Unindexed Flat Images:** The original website had virtually zero accessible HTML text. All brochure pages were compiled as raster JPEGs (`p21.jpg` – `p8.jpg`), destroying SEO indexing, mobile responsiveness, accessibility, and search ranking.
2. **Hidden High-Value Assets:** The `SupplyList.pdf` contained multi-billion-dollar client credentials (Aramco, ADNOC, Bechtel, Petrofac), 40+ international manufacturers, and deep technical specs, yet it was buried behind an unstyled, easily missed hyperlink.
3. **Typography & Grammatical Slips:**
   - Phrasing such as _"executed numerous of prestigious projects"_ should be elevated to _"successfully executed numerous prestigious flagship projects"_.
   - In the HSE policy, _"improving the competency level of employs"_ was a clear typo for _"employees"_.
   - Phrasing such as _"Execution of electric"_ in retail slides should be modernized to _"Turnkey High & Low Voltage Electrical Infrastructure"_.
